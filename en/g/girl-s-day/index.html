Girl's Day is a 4-member K-pop girl group under Dream T Entertainment, debuting in 2010. Fan club: DAI5Y. Recent releases: GIRL'S DAY EVERYDAY #5 (2017), Girl's Day Love Second Album (2015), Hello Bubble (2015).
